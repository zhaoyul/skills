# Translation quality gates

Final semantic review should explicitly sample or exhaustively check:

1. chapter and section titles, especially puns;
2. terminology consistency;
3. numbers, percentages, fold changes and units;
4. negation and scope;
5. causal and comparative language;
6. quotes and cited claims;
7. humor, irony and rhetorical questions;
8. paragraphs with high residual English;
9. OCR-derived text;
10. captions and text adjacent to formulas.

Do not use global string replacement as a substitute for semantic editing.
